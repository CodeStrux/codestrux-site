# What we have built

A look at the products we design, build, and operate. These are our own
projects — the work shows the range of the stack we cover, from messaging
platforms to e-invoicing to developer tooling.

<div class="card-grid" markdown="1">

<div class="card" markdown="1">
### Stratos WhatsApp
<p class="card-meta">Audio-first WhatsApp Business inbox for LATAM SMBs</p>

A real-time WhatsApp Business inbox built for small and mid-sized businesses in
Latin America. It delivers messages over SSE, transcribes Spanish voice notes,
suggests replies, and reconciles payment receipts (SINPE, Pix, SPEI) with OCR.
It supports templates with variables, WhatsApp Flows, vertical AI agents, and
consent/opt-in compliance for Costa Rica's Ley 8968, Brazil's LGPD, and Mexico's
LFPDPPP.

*Stack: Next.js front end, Java 21 / Spring Boot services, MySQL and MongoDB.*
</div>

<div class="card" markdown="1">
### FacturaCR (ce-je)
<p class="card-meta">Costa Rican electronic invoicing</p>

A Costa Rican electronic-invoicing engine. It generates, signs (XAdES), and
submits comprobantes to Hacienda against the v4.4 XSD — facturas, tiquetes,
notas de crédito and débito, compras, exportación, and recibos — with export to
PDF, Excel, and QR.

*Stack: Jakarta EE 10, Java 21, WildFly, EclipseLink, RESTEasy/Jersey,
JSF/PrimeFaces, JAXB, MySQL/Percona.*
</div>

<div class="card" markdown="1">
### Aether
<p class="card-meta">Open multi-agent orchestration framework</p>

An open framework for orchestrating multiple Claude Code sessions: a filesystem
relay protocol between parallel, persona'd agents, with plan / brief / report
contracts, reproducible machine bootstrap, and deterministic, script-rendered
reports. Released under the CodeStrux organization on GitHub.

*Stack: filesystem relay protocol, persona'd agent sessions, reproducible
bootstrap, script-rendered reporting.*
</div>
