<section class="pane pane--col-2" aria-label="How the pod works">
  <div class="pane__bar"><span class="pane__path">fde/loop</span><span class="pane__dots" aria-hidden="true"><i class="dot dot--a"></i><i class="dot dot--b"></i><i class="dot dot--c"></i></span></div>
  <div class="pane__body">
    <h2 class="cmd-head"><span class="sigil">$</span> loop</h2>
    <p>Work runs as propose → improve → execute. Grounded options come from the systems and the domain; deep review sharpens the plan; then the team ships. You get a production outcome — and durable leave-behinds so the next step is not a rebuild from zero.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="pane pane--col-4" aria-label="Engagement path">
  <div class="pane__bar"><span class="pane__path">fde/path</span><span class="pane__dots" aria-hidden="true"><i class="dot dot--a"></i><i class="dot dot--b"></i><i class="dot dot--c"></i></span></div>
  <div class="pane__body">
    <h2 class="cmd-head"><span class="sigil">$</span> path <span class="flag">--bounded</span></h2>
    <p>Engagements stay bounded on roughly a quarter-scale cadence: scope the problem worth solving, prove it on a prototype with real data, harden into production, then hand off to named owners on your side. Repository and CI access on day one. A handoff date is part of the deal — not an open-ended embed.</p>
  </div>
</section>
