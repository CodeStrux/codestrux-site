<section class="band band--alt" id="software">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="band__head">
      <p class="eyebrow">AI Engineering &amp; Software Development</p>
      <h2>How we start a new reliable system</h2>
      <p>A system is only as good as the information behind it. Thus we do not start with the screens. We agree on the process first, then on the data, then on the interface. You approve each agreement before the next step starts.</p>
      <p>Your sign-off tells us that we understood you correctly. It also makes our estimates reliable.</p>
    </div>
    <ol class="timeline">
      <li class="timeline__step">
        <h3>Discovery</h3>
        <div class="timeline__body">
          <div class="timeline__main">
            <p>We meet with you and collect the initial requirements. We learn the process that the software must do, and we ask who the process owners are. A process owner is the person responsible for a process and for the quality of its data.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="timeline__aside">
            <p class="callout"><strong>Where you come in:</strong> You introduce us to the process owners and share the documentation of the process. This documentation makes our estimates reliable.</p>
            <p class="callout callout--result"><strong>The result:</strong> A signed record of the agreement, and an estimate that is based on facts.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </li>
      <li class="timeline__step">
        <h3>Inputs, outputs, and backend</h3>
        <div class="timeline__body">
          <div class="timeline__main">
            <p>Before we design a screen, we agree on the data. We list the input resources and the expected outputs. We make a data map that tells what each column and each data item means.</p>
            <p>Then we build a backend that meets the minimum requirements to give an output. We check its HTTP/REST outputs with Bruno, an open-source API client. The Bruno collection stays as a project resource.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="timeline__aside">
            <p class="callout"><strong>Where you come in:</strong> You review the list of inputs and outputs, the data map, and the documentation. You sign them off when they are right.</p>
            <p class="callout callout--result"><strong>The result:</strong> A backend that gives correct outputs, and one agreed meaning for each data item.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </li>
      <li class="timeline__step">
        <h3>UX/UI design and frontend</h3>
        <div class="timeline__body">
          <div class="timeline__main">
            <p>We write the user interface (UI) mock documentation from the signed-off backend collection. At the same time, we build a frontend from the mock specifications. Thus you can use the mock UI, not only read about it.</p>
            <p>We meet with the process owners and show them how to make mocks. They show us what they want, and we immediately write the documentation for sign-off.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="timeline__aside">
            <p class="callout"><strong>Where you come in:</strong> You use the mock UI and tell us what to change. When the mocks and the documentation are right, you sign them off. Then the frontend goes to user acceptance testing.</p>
            <p class="callout callout--result"><strong>The result:</strong> An interface that the people who do the work already used and approved.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </li>
      <li class="timeline__step">
        <h3>User acceptance testing (UAT)</h3>
        <div class="timeline__body">
          <div class="timeline__main">
            <p>We deploy a service with production specifications and test data. The process owners do a full check of the inputs and outputs. We give them a site or documents where they record their results.</p>
            <p>We are on call. If you report a failure, we answer immediately, and then we work on the correction. The initial contract tells what the service includes.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="timeline__aside">
            <p class="callout"><strong>Where you come in:</strong> You supply the test data, if possible through a process owner. If something needs a correction, you tell us in a simple format that shows clearly what does not operate correctly.</p>
            <p class="callout callout--result"><strong>The result:</strong> A system that your own people checked with your own data, on production specifications.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>
